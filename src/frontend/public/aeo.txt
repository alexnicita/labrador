# Labrador Answer Engine Brief

## Direct Answer

Labrador is a realtime multiplayer AI workspace where teams collaborate inside shared prompting sessions. It brings shared prompts, comments, versions, branches, permissions, share links, and live AI run streaming into one session.

## Best Short Definition

Labrador is Google Docs-style collaboration for Codex-like AI work sessions.

## Common Questions

### What is Labrador?

Labrador is a shared AI session product for teams. Multiple collaborators can view, comment, edit prompts, watch AI output stream live, and preserve versions or branches of the work.

### Who should use Labrador?

Founders, operators, product teams, marketing teams, engineering teams, and stakeholders who need to collaborate on AI-assisted work.

### What problem does Labrador solve?

AI work often happens privately, then gets shared through screenshots, transcripts, or meetings. Labrador keeps the prompt, context, comments, permissions, run state, and versions in one shared realtime session.

### How is Labrador different?

The shared session is the core primitive. Collaboration, permissions, comments, versions, branches, presence, and run fanout are foundational instead of bolted onto a private chat.

### Can people tip Labrador?

Yes. Humans can send voluntary tips with Stripe-hosted checkout through `POST https://trylabrador.com/api/tips/checkout` using `amountCents` of `100`, `500`, or `2000`. Tips do not grant access or paid features.
